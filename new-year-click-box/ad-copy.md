## Pain Point → https://khanh97hg.github.io/landers/new-year-click-box/pain.html
Image ad: `ads/img/pain-native-16x9.webp`

### Hooks
1. The New Year gift list is long again. Take a one-minute break.
2. Tired of fake prize links? Here's what a fair giveaway looks like.
3. Busy December? A free New Year game that takes a minute.

### PAS body
**Pain:** December piles up: gifts for family, the office exchange, the kids, and a phone full of prize
messages you can't trust. **Agitate:** Most of those "you won" links ask for a fee or your details, so you've
stopped trusting any giveaway at all. **Solution:** A free New Year gift box game: open a box, enter a fair
giveaway under the sponsor's rules, and get back to your evening. Free to play, no guaranteed prize.

### Native headlines (≤60 characters)
1. The New Year Gift List Is Long Again. Try This Free Game (56)
2. A Real Giveaway Never Asks You to Pay. Here's One to Try (56)
3. Busy December? Open a Free New Year Gift Box (44)
4. Fake Prize Links Everywhere? How a Fair Giveaway Works (54)
5. End the Year on a Lighter Note: Open a Gift Box (47)

### Native descriptions / brand line (Adsterra optional fields)
- Free to play. A chance to win, no guaranteed prize.
- Brand name: The Festive Feed
