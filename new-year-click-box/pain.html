What a fair giveaway looks like

Honest online giveaways work the same way the old mall raffles did: you enter for free, the sponsor publishes the rules, and winners are picked under those rules. The New Year gift box game is built like that. You tap to open a box, then you can enter the giveaway.

Scam prize links

  • A "prize" for something you never entered
  • Asks for a fee to release the prize
  • Pretends to be a store, bank or agency

A fair giveaway

  • Free to enter, nothing to pay
  • A chance to win, clearly stated
  • Sponsor's official rules to read

Before you enter any giveaway

  • Check that entering is free. If you are asked to pay to collect a prize, close the page.
  • Read the sponsor's rules: who can enter, when it ends, how winners are picked.
  • Never share bank PINs, one-time codes or passwords. No real giveaway needs them.
