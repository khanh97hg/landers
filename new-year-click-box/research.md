# Research: Playable | New Year Click Box (Zeydoo, CPL), Adsterra Native Banner

## 1. Offer check
- **Offer:** Playable | New Year Click Box (Zeydoo offer ID 27274). A Zeydoo "Interactive / Playable" CPL
  offer: the visitor taps open a virtual gift box (a short mini-game with a New Year theme) and then reaches
  a sweepstakes / giveaway registration step. Zeydoo files Click Box under its giveaway / sweepstakes
  interactive offers. Conversion type: **CPL** (pays on the lead / registration, not on a purchase).
- **Geo:** worldwide (237 countries in the offer's targeting, all devices). Payout tiers on this account:
  $0.70 (US, CA, GB, AU, NZ), $0.35 (Western Europe, Gulf, SG, HK, ZA), **$0.25** (the payout given), $0.08
  and $0.04 tiers. The $0.25 tier is: AO AR AZ BD BG BH BR BY CD CI CL CM CY CZ EE GH GL GN GR HR HU ID IQ IR JO
  KE KR KZ LB LT LV MA MC MD ME MK ML MT MW MX MY MZ OM PH PL PT QA RO RS SI SK TH TR VN ZM UA.
  **The pages are in English**, so start with the English-reading countries in that tier: **Philippines,
  Malaysia, Kenya, Ghana** (then Malta, Cyprus, Zambia, Malawi). Other $0.25 countries need translated pages.
- **Slug:** `new-year-click-box`
- **Vertical:** Other, run as **Sweepstakes** for the legal lines (giveaway). Sweepstakes footer line on every
  page and in terms.html.
- **EPC / payout:** $0.25 (as given, CPL). Information only.
- **Affiliate link (BeMob campaign URL):**
  `https://fc8oa.bemobtrcks.com/go/6665c149-7980-4de3-9519-045ab5773c7c?subid=##SUB_ID_SHORT(action)##&placement_id=##PLACEMENT_ID##&campaign_id=##CAMPAIGN_ID##&banner_id=##BANNER_ID##&country_code=##COUNTRY_CODE##&device_brand=##DEVICE_BRAND##&browser_name=##BROWSER_NAME##&user_os=##USER_OS##&remote_country=##REMOTE_COUNTRY##&remote_language=##REMOTE_LANGUAGE##&useragent=##USERAGENT##`
  The `##...##` parts are **Adsterra macros**. Adsterra only fills them in the URL of the ad itself, not in
  links on a page it sends people to. So with a lander in between:
  - Every CTA uses the clean BeMob URL plus the angle tag:
    `https://fc8oa.bemobtrcks.com/go/6665c149-7980-4de3-9519-045ab5773c7c?sub=<angle>`
  - The **Adsterra ad's target URL** is the lander with the macros on it, for example
    `https://khanh97hg.github.io/landers/new-year-click-box/pain.html?subid=##SUB_ID_SHORT(action)##&placement_id=##PLACEMENT_ID##&campaign_id=##CAMPAIGN_ID##&banner_id=##BANNER_ID##&country_code=##COUNTRY_CODE##&device_brand=##DEVICE_BRAND##&browser_name=##BROWSER_NAME##&user_os=##USER_OS##&remote_country=##REMOTE_COUNTRY##&remote_language=##REMOTE_LANGUAGE##&useragent=##USERAGENT##`
  - The pages' tracking pass-through script copies all of those parameters onto the BeMob link (the
    parameter list was extended for this campaign), so BeMob still gets the Adsterra sub ID for postbacks
    and every placement / country / device value.
  - BeMob alternative: if the BeMob campaign is set up with a landing page, use BeMob's click URL as the CTA
    instead (`/swap-link`).
- **Traffic:** Adsterra **Native Banner** (overrides the Facebook + Native default). Mostly mobile, low-intent
  readers on publisher sites; creatives are a small image plus a short title.
- **Test budget:** $50 split across 3 angles (~$16.67 each), one Adsterra campaign per angle.

### Adsterra policy check: allowed, with restrictions
Adsterra lists sweepstakes, including giveaway sweepstakes, as a supported vertical and names native and
display banners as formats for it. Its creative rules are general: nothing misleading, no malware, no forced
redirects or unexpected downloads. Zeydoo's offer rules (allowed sources, incentive rules) also apply:
check the offer page in Zeydoo for any banned traffic types before launch. What the pages and ads avoid:
- No "You won!", "Congratulations, you've been selected", fake system alerts or fake notification UI.
- No guaranteed prize, no specific prize named (the offer page decides what the giveaway is), no odds.
- No fake countdowns or "only X left"; the New Year theme is the only timing.
- No impersonating a brand, telecom, bank or government giveaway (a common scam pattern in PH).
- The pages say plainly: a chance to win, no guaranteed prize, follow the sponsor's official rules,
  a real giveaway never asks you to pay to collect a prize.
- Native line in the footer and "Advertisement · Advertorial" on the advertorial.

## 2. Avatar
1. **Who:** adults 18–45 in the Philippines, Malaysia, Kenya and Ghana, reading on an Android phone on mobile
   data. Students, young workers, parents. They browse news, entertainment and download sites in short bursts
   (commute, breaks, late evening). In late November–December the season is on their mind: Christmas and New
   Year gifts, family gatherings, end-of-year spending.
2. **Top 10 pain points:**
   1. A long gift list for family and friends at the end of the year.
   2. The season gets expensive all at once (gifts, food, travel home).
   3. Online "giveaways" that turn out to be scams asking for a fee or personal codes.
   4. Fake prize texts and Messenger links pretending to be a telecom, a store or a government agency.
   5. Giveaways that are really endless surveys with no clear end.
   6. Pages full of pop-ups and fake "you won" alerts.
   7. Not knowing whether a promo is real or who runs it.
   8. Slow pages that eat mobile data.
   9. Boredom in idle moments; wanting something light and fun.
   10. Feeling that only other people ever win anything.
3. **Already tried:** Facebook page "share to win" posts, raffle tickets, mall and store promos, text
   promos, spin-the-wheel apps, survey sites that never finished.
4. **What makes them click now:** the New Year mood, a light game (open a box) instead of a form, a chance at a
   gift with no cost to join, and a page that is honest about how giveaways work.
5. **Where they spend time online:** Facebook and Messenger, TikTok, YouTube, news and entertainment portals,
   free download and streaming sites (where Adsterra native widgets run), mobile games.

**Competitor notes:** sweepstakes on pop / native networks lean on "You won", fake system alerts and fake
countdowns. Those burn out quickly and get flagged. Zeydoo's own case studies show Click Box working on push,
pop and social with a simple funnel; a light, honest pre-lander that sets the game up ("open a box") fits the
playable format.

**Market context (planning only, not used as claims):** Philippine agencies (DSWD, CICC) and press keep warning
about fake prize surveys and rewards links that ask for fees or personal data. A page that says "a real
giveaway never asks you to pay to collect a prize" earns trust with this audience.

## 3. Angle scores (cold, mobile native-banner reader)
| Angle | Score | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Curiosity | 5 | "Which box would you open?" is an open loop the playable itself closes; a 3-tap quiz is almost the game already. |
| Direct Benefit | 4 | Simple end state: a fun New Year game, free to join, a chance at a gift. Works as a fast, low-effort page. |
| Pain Point | 4 | The end-of-year gift list is a real, shared pressure in these markets, and giveaway scams are a real frustration; an honest story builds trust. |
| Fear | 2 | The only factual fear is prize scams; leading with scams and then sending people to a giveaway reads as contradictory. Kept as a trust line inside the Pain page instead. |
| Social Proof | 1 | No winner numbers or reviews published by the offer owner. Guardrail: no proof → skip. Never invent winners. |

**Picked:** Curiosity (5), Direct Benefit (4), Pain Point (4).

**Skipped:**
- **Social Proof:** fails the guardrail (no offer-owner proof). Never show invented winners.
- **Fear:** weak and self-defeating for a giveaway; the scam-awareness point lives on the Pain page as a fact.

## 4. Formats
Sweepstakes isn't in the niche table; the closest fit is a low-effort sign-up. Native expects an Advertorial,
and a quiz is the lowest-effort format for a playable giveaway. Two formats: Advertorial and Quiz.

| Page | Angle | Format | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `pain.html` | Pain Point | Advertorial | Pain is a story (the long gift list, the scam links); native readers expect an article, and it has room to explain how a real giveaway works. Kept short for mobile. |
| `curiosity.html` | Curiosity | Quiz | "Which gift box would you open first?" as a picture grid is the open loop, and the quiz flows straight into the box-opening game. |
| `benefit.html` | Direct Benefit | Quiz | Direct is built for search intent; on cheap mobile native traffic the benefit lands best as 3 quick taps about the New Year, then the game. |

Quiz questions (no ZIP: the offer isn't location-based):
- Curiosity: which gift box would you open first (picture grid) → how you'll spend New Year's Eve → what you'd
  love to unwrap → age range.
- Benefit: how you'll welcome the New Year → what's still on your gift list → how you feel about games on your phone → age range.
