# Ad Copy: Surveoo (Facebook)

Traffic source: **Facebook (Meta: Facebook + Instagram)**, one ad set per angle, $50 split equally (~$16.67 each), each pointing at its own page.
Headlines are 40 characters max and descriptions 30 max (checked). Hooks are under 15 words.

**Before launch (Meta income-claims rules):** no amounts, no "make money" or "easy cash" promises, no cash or lifestyle images, and no lines that assert facts about the viewer's finances ("Need extra cash?"). Every line below describes the product or a scenario. No Special Ad Category applies.

## Pain Point (Advertorial)

**Landing page:** https://khanh97hg.github.io/landers/surveoo/pain.html

### Hooks

1. Screened out at minute 15? There's a less frustrating way to do surveys.
2. Survey apps that pay in points you can't read? Try cash value.
3. One screen-out shouldn't end your whole survey session.

### PAS body

Survey apps promise "about 5 minutes" and screen you out at minute 15. Then there are the points you can't value, a cash-out minimum that feels far off, and nothing else to try, so one screen-out ends the session. A free survey site that pulls surveys from many research panels into one account gives you another survey to try, with your balance shown in cash value. Join free, no card needed. You won't qualify for every survey.

### Meta / Facebook: primary text

**Short:** Screened out after 15 minutes? A survey site that pulls from many research panels gives you another survey to try. Free to join.

**Medium:** Most survey frustration isn't you. Each study needs a specific group of people, so screen-outs happen on every site. The difference is what comes next: on a site that gathers surveys from many panels, there's another one to try. Rewards show in cash value, not points. Free to join, no card needed.

**Story (second-person scenario):** You've got 20 minutes on the train, so you open a survey app. The survey says "about 5 minutes." Twelve minutes in, you're still answering questions about laundry detergent. Then: "Sorry, you don't match this survey." A few points, and no idea what they're worth. There's a less frustrating setup: one free account that pulls surveys from many research panels, so a screen-out just means trying the next one, and a balance shown in cash value. You won't qualify for every survey. Nobody does.

## Direct Benefit (Quiz)

**Landing page:** https://khanh97hg.github.io/landers/surveoo/benefit.html

### Hooks

1. Spare minutes on the bus? Make them paid surveys.
2. Paid surveys in cash value, not points. Free to join.
3. Surveys from many research panels, in one free account.

### PAS body

Waiting rooms, commutes, lunch breaks: spare minutes usually end up as scrolling. And the survey apps that do pay often use points you can't value and run out of surveys fast. One free account gives you surveys from many panels, a balance shown in cash value, and cash-out by PayPal, bank transfer or gift card. Results vary, and you won't qualify for every survey.

### Meta / Facebook: primary text

**Short:** Paid surveys for your spare minutes. Cash value, not points. Free to join, no card needed.

**Medium:** One free account, surveys from many research panels, and a balance you can actually read because it's shown in cash value, not points. Take surveys on your phone or laptop when you have a few minutes, and cash out by PayPal, bank transfer or gift card. You won't qualify for every survey; results vary.

**Story (second-person scenario):** You're in a waiting room with fifteen minutes and your phone. Normally that's scrolling. Instead you open a survey site, pick a survey about grocery shopping, and answer questions while you wait. Screened out? You pick another. Your balance shows in cash value, so you know where you stand, and you choose PayPal, bank transfer or gift cards when you cash out. Free to join, no card needed. Results vary.
